Three Word2Vec models, one each for Reagan, Obama, and Trump, trained on their State of the Union, inaugural, and farewell speeches. Word2Vec learns which words appear near each other, so it can tell which words a speaker uses in similar contexts. Given a word such as "economy", the app lists the five closest words for each president and adds a short AI-written interpretation of each list. It was a final project for Math 356, Statistical Methods in Machine Learning, at Hamilton College.
