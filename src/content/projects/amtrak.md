Over one winter break a friend and I circled the country on an [Amtrak rail pass](https://www.amtrak.com/usa-rail-pass), which allows ten rides in thirty days. We went down the East Coast to Florida, across the South from New Orleans to Los Angeles, up the Pacific coast to Seattle, and back through Minneapolis and Chicago: twelve legs in all, starting and ending in Philadelphia. The longest single ride, New Orleans to Los Angeles, takes about 48 hours.
