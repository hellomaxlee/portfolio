# portfolio: Project Notes

## Sources
- Max's public LinkedIn profile: the career facts.
- Max's public GitHub repositories: every project description.
- Max's own travel write-ups: every side-quest number.
- Max's 2022 capstone script for his summer at the Rhode Island refuges: Summer in the Woods.

## Critical assumptions
- Every number on the page traces to one of the sources above. Where two sources disagreed the
  number was left out.
- Only public projects are shown.
- Map interchanges are true by place: Stockholm (semester abroad, European Excursions),
  Washington (a summer at the CFPB, the Smithsonian visits), New York (work, StationScope,
  Hide and Seek). The map carries no dates.
- The Life Dashboard strip is rendered from that project's sample fixture, not from real data.
- The portrait is Max's GitHub profile picture, copied to `public/max.jpg`.

## Design decisions
- A quiet editorial page. Newsreader for headings and numerals, Instrument Sans for text.
- One deep pine-green ground carries the hero, the footer, and the featured card. A three-line network map (work, things built,
  side quests) is the single themed element, and the quest line continues down the side-quest list.
- Colour: nature tones (Max, 2026-11-17: the indigo and neon set read as a default template).
  Wheat, terracotta, and lake blue for the three lines on pine green; cream paper and bark ink.
  All text pairs measure 4.8:1 or better. The wheat section swatch on paper is 2.4:1; it is
  decorative and its ink ring carries the mark.
- The side-quest line runs on two tracks and changes track between entries, using the hero map's
  45 degree bends with rounded corners. The text keeps one left edge; only the line and stations
  move. The line opens with a lead-in bend and closes with a terminus bar. An entry reached from
  the map (`:target`) fills its station in lake blue.
- A thin dashed fork of the side-quest line leads to Max's cooking account on Instagram
  (@byemaxlee; Max, 2026-11-18). It is kept subtle: short, thinner than the line, a smaller
  station, muted type, no legend entry, and an end of its own that touches no other line. On the
  hero map it leaves Stockholm and ends in the open space under the side-quest stops (to the left
  of the trunk on a phone). Beside the side-quest list it forks left at the top of the line,
  drops down the gap between the columns, and turns left on a 45 degree bend to run under the
  intro, ending at a stop whose label keeps the column's left edge; on a phone, where there is no
  room to the left, it sits in the row above the list. No photo: thumbnails were tried and were
  louder than the entries around them.
- Vertical rhythm is tight (Max, 2026-11-19: too much white space). The gap between sections is
  `--space-8`, 40 px on a phone rising to 72 px on a wide screen; spacing inside sections is
  unchanged.
- On wide screens (56rem and up) the side-quest entries sit either side of the line (Max,
  2026-11-19: the column under the heading was empty). Each entry's `side` is set in its file and
  defaults to right; Holland and the Smithsonian are on the left, with right-aligned text, because
  that split leaves the two columns closest to even. Each station is on the track nearest its
  entry. It is built with CSS subgrid; browsers without subgrid, and phones, keep one column.
- "A Summer in the Woods" is the first side quest. It is told as a side quest, not as work
  experience: the house in the woods, the trail, the books, the oatmeal (Max, 2026-11-19). The
  capstone video is unlisted and is not linked.
- The side-quest line now starts at Summer in the Woods, and the cooking fork leaves it there, so
  cooking comes before every stop.
- Only cards whose number says something carry one. StationScope is the featured card.
- Career appears as two short paragraphs, not a list. The employer is named once.
- Self-contained: outside links are LinkedIn, GitHub, Max's own apps, his cooking account on
  Instagram, and the organisations named in the work paragraph.
- No client JavaScript. All motion is CSS and stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The site is served from `/portfolio` on GitHub Pages; internal URLs use `BASE_URL`.
